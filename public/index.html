Building dependable production AI systems used by real teams.

I graduated with a Bachelor's in Computer Science from Hasanuddin University with Cum Laude honors (3.97/4.00 GPA) — focusing on building reliable AI and software architectures that deliver tangible value in production environments, prioritizing robustness, low latency, and dependable pipelines over superficial demos.

That engineering discipline has guided my work across industry and research. At Paper.id, I helped ship an enterprise OCR pipeline for KYC/KYB document verification achieving 99% accuracy with a 4-second turnaround by engineering resilient, production-grade infrastructure. At XL Axiata, I built computer vision systems for road damage and safety-equipment detection that had to work on a Raspberry Pi in the field, not just a GPU in a notebook. Today, as AI Trainer at RevoU and Co-Founder of AksaraHub Nusantara, I split my time between teaching organizations how to use AI responsibly and building intelligent tools that solve complex challenges.

Ship, then refine

A model in production teaching you real lessons beats a model in a notebook proving a hypothesis.

Systems over demos

Accuracy numbers mean little without latency, cost, and failure modes accounted for.

Teach as you build

Explaining a system to a non-technical stakeholder is the fastest way to find its weak points.

Technology in service of culture

Some of my favorite work uses AI to preserve, not replace — local scripts, languages, heritage.
